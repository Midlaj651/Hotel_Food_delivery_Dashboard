
# 🍔 Food Delivery Analytics Dashboard | Power BI

## 📌 Project Overview

The Food Delivery Analytics Dashboard is an interactive Power BI project designed to analyze food delivery operations, order performance, profitability, delivery costs, customer ratings, and customer ordering patterns.

The dashboard helps understand business performance through interactive visualizations and key performance indicators (KPIs).

## 🎯 Project Objectives

- Analyze total food delivery orders.
- Monitor total order profit and order value.
- Analyze delivery costs over time.
- Identify profitable delivery zones.
- Understand customer ratings.
- Analyze order patterns by meal period.
- Explore the impact of weather conditions on orders.
- Compare weekday and weekend order activity.

## 📊 Key Performance Indicators (KPIs)

- **Total Orders:** 5K
- **Total Order Profit:** 2.49M
- **Total Delivery Zones:** 20
- **Total Riders:** 60
- **Total Order Value:** 380.56M
- **Average Customer Rating:** 3.00

*Note: KPI values are based on the dashboard screenshot and may be rounded.*

## 📈 Dashboard Visualizations

The dashboard includes the following visualizations:

1. **Executive Overview** – Summary of key business performance indicators.
2. **Order Profit and Order Value Trends** – Monthly trends in order profitability and order value.
3. **Delivery Cost Analysis** – Delivery cost trends by year and month.
4. **Profit by Delivery Zone** – Comparison of order profit across delivery zones.
5. **Orders by Meal Period** – Analysis of orders across different meal periods.
6. **Orders by Weather Condition** – Comparison of order counts under different weather conditions.
7. **Weekend vs. Weekday Orders** – Analysis of order distribution based on weekends.
8. **Customer Rating Analysis** – Overview of average customer ratings.
